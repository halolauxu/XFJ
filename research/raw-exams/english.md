> Last summer, Kelly went to a fishing village for vacation with her parents. As soon as she arrived, she ran to the beach with her watercolors, brushes and paper. Fishermen were busy fishing. Seabirds were flying around. Just then a red-haired girl passed by. "Hello there!" said Kelly. "Hello," said the girl, but she didn't stop. In fact, she seemed to walk faster. Kelly was unhappy. She thought it would be boring if she couldn't make any friends in the village.
>
> After a while, Kelly climbed upon the rocks and began to paint. She was painting a blue band (条纹) when she heard someone climbing up the rock behind her. She knew her parents were on the beach below.

- 体裁：记叙文
- 题型：4 选 1（细节 + 推理 + 主旨）

### 二、完形填空（题 21-30，共 10 分）

**原文**：Jakey 与小刺猬

> One day, when Jakey went outside to play, he found a hedgehog in a window well. It seemed that the hedgehog couldn't get out. Jakey's big brothers, Artie and Danny, came around and noticed the hedgehog too. "We need to get it out!" they said and hurried to their room.
>
> When Jakey followed them, he found Artie and Danny were making plans. "We can't touch the hedgehog. It might bite," Artie said. "A rope may be helpful." "We'll also need a platform." Danny added and explained their plan to Jakey.
>
> When asked how they would get the hedgehog to walk onto the platform, Artie suggested an old beef sandwich. Jakey looked at it and thought the hedgehog would get sick after eating it. So he decided to find his own way to help the hedgehog.
>
> Jakey searched around the house and luckily found a nice long board. He pulled the board and placed it against a corner of the well, next to the hedgehog. After placing the board, he climbed up into his tree house and waited quietly. A few minutes later, Jakey saw the hedgehog smell the board, touch it with one foot, and walk up the board. It got out of the well finally and disappeared into the trees.

- 题型：4 选 1
- 主题：救助小刺猬 / 同理心 / 解决问题
- 答案：题文与选项尚未抓到（10 题）
- 考点（推断）：动词、形容词、副词、连词；上下文照应

### 三、语法填空 / 语篇填空（题 31-40，共 10 分）

**已知主题**：Wallball（壁球）这项运动

> Wallball is a sport that only requires a ball and a wall. You open your hand and use your palm to hit the ball. Gloves are usually needed (but optional). You can use either hand to hit the ball, and two players take turns hitting the ball.

- 3 个无提示词填空（虚词：介词/冠词/连词）

- 答案待补
- **重要考点**：take turns to do、hit the ball、动词不定式 vs 动名词
